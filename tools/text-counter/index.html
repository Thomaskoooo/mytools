About this tool

The text counter analyzes your text as you type, giving instant counts of characters, words, sentences, lines and paragraphs. It's useful for meeting word-count limits on essays and social posts, checking character limits for tweets or meta descriptions, or just getting a quick sense of a document's length. Everything is computed locally in your browser — your text is never sent anywhere.
